Answers for donors and NGOs

Frequently asked questions.

Learn what to expect when posting surplus food or coordinating a collection.

Who can create a food listing?

Food donors such as restaurants, hotels, bakeries, supermarkets, caterers, and event venues can create an account and post eligible surplus.

How do NGOs find a donation?

Active NGO accounts can browse available listings, review details and deadlines, and accept a donation they can collect.

Can I post food late at night?

Yes. Listings are not rejected because of the hour. Choose a realistic pickup time and collection deadline; expired listings are excluded from available listings.

Does the freshness estimate certify that food is safe?

No. It only reflects the entered timing and donor-reported handling details. It cannot inspect or certify food. Recipients should assess the food and handling conditions at collection.

What if I need to cancel a donation?

Donors can cancel an unclaimed listing. The cancelled donation remains in the donor's history. Accepted donations must be coordinated with the NGO.

Why is an NGO account waiting for approval?

New NGO applications require administrator review before sign-in is enabled. The registration page confirms when an application has been submitted.

Why did I not receive a sign-in code?

Check your spam folder and verify the email address. If the app reports a delivery error, the email provider may need account activation and a verified sender domain.

Can I sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple, or Facebook?

Those OAuth providers are not currently configured for this project. Use email and password sign-in instead.
