Our mission

Good food should find its way to people.

Food Rescue helps food donors and community organizations coordinate surplus food recovery with clear listings, pickup deadlines, and direct communication.

Become a donorJoin as an NGO

Reduce avoidable waste

Donors can post eligible surplus with preparation details, storage declarations, pickup location, and a collection deadline so nearby NGOs can decide whether they can collect it.

Coordinate locally

NGOs browse current listings, accept a donation they can collect, and keep the donor updated through the platform's status and messaging workflow.

Put communities first

The platform supports local handoffs between food businesses and organizations serving their communities. Each rescue depends on a willing donor and an available recipient.

Handle food responsibly

Storage and safety declarations are provided by the donor. Timing estimates help with coordination; recipients should inspect food and handling conditions at collection.
