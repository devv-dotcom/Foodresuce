A clear local handoff

From surplus to a coordinated collection.

Donors and NGOs use one shared listing to agree on food details, location, and pickup timing.

1. Donor posts food

Enter the food name, category, quantity, preparation time, deadline, storage condition, pickup address, and donor handling declarations. Add a photo and choose a pickup time within the listed window.

2. NGO reviews listings

Eligible NGOs browse available, unexpired listings and check quantity, pickup details, timing, and donor-provided handling information.

3. NGO accepts

An active NGO accepts an available donation. The platform prevents a second organization from claiming the same listing.

4. Coordinate collection

The donor and NGO use the listing and conversation tools to coordinate pickup. They should independently assess whether the food and its handling are acceptable.

5. Confirm completion

The NGO confirms when distribution is complete. Donors and NGOs can review completed and cancelled items in their histories.

Late-night listings

Listings can be created at any hour. Set realistic pickup and safe collection deadlines; expired listings are not shown as available to NGOs.
